My Voice Matters

Planned capability

MaternalGuard is being designed to help mothers document concerns and communicate with their healthcare teams. Concern reporting and clinical follow-up are not available in this version.

Your Care. Your Voice.

Future versions aim to help mothers:

  • Document health concerns.
  • Communicate with their care teams.
  • Track responses and follow-up.
  • Access their maternal health information.

These are planned capabilities. This version displays available patient information and observations only.

A note about your care

MaternalGuard provides access to health information available in your record. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional medical advice, and it is not continuously monitored by healthcare professionals. If you are experiencing severe or urgent symptoms, seek immediate medical attention rather than waiting for an application response.
